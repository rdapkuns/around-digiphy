Adjustable seats

In Digiphy, the seats are fully adjustable. I can change their dimensions and angles. Overall feel to match whatever interior I'm designing. Whether the project needs something sporty, compact, or more relaxed, I just shape the seat to fit the car's character, make them and drop it straight into DigiPHY.

Steering wheel

The steering wheel in Digiphy is fully adjustable, so I can match it to any type of car I'm developing. I can shift the height, widen the stance, or tighten it up depending on whatever I'm working on.

Alignment cameras

The alignment cameras help me bring the digital layer into the real setup. They make sure every virtual element sits exactly where it should, so the whole experience lines up perfectly.

Pedals

The pedals in Digiphy are fully adjustable, so I can set them up to match the driving position of any type of car. I can move them closer, lower, wider... Whatever the layout needs.

CONCEPT STATION

A new design always begins with sketching. It's the quickest way to explore ideas and see what works. Once an idea feels right on paper, I want to understand how it behaves in space and how the user interacts with it. That's the moment when DigiPHY takes over.

Here you can select which components you want to use and customize them to match your preferred setup.

Adding accessories

When I design a car, the physical experience matters as much as the digital one. That's why DigiPHY uses real, physical 3D printed or modelled add-ons that I can mount onto the frame to see how they fit and feel.
